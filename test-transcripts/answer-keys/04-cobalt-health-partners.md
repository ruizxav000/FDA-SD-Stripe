# 04 — Cobalt Health Partners — Answer key

**Scenario:** A loss to Ledgerpoint. The AE has a theory about what the competitor said, but the customer never said it.
**What it tests:** Your rule for competitive intel: *treat it as opinion unless the rep quotes the customer; "I don't know" is a valid answer.* The real signal is **how their questions changed**.

**Snapshot:** Outcome **Lost** · Competitor **Ledgerpoint** · AE Lena Vogel · Industry: healthcare, 12 clinics, each its own legal entity

## The seven insights

| # | Insight | Expected finding | Evidence grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What started it | **ERP migration from QuickBooks to NetSuite** (kicked off in January). The controller wanted a close tool chosen before NetSuite went live. | Customer said |
| 2 | Who decided | **Anita Rao, CFO.** Ben (controller): *"Anita made the call, it was hers."* Anita attended two calls. | Customer said |
| 3 | Competitor | What the customer brought up: **essentially nothing until the end. The AE said "I don't know."** The one direct quote, from Ben on the final call: *"Ledgerpoint showed us our consolidation running in our NetSuite sandbox."* **What changed after their demo (week 5):** questions shifted from the close checklist to **intercompany eliminations and NetSuite sync depth** (subsidiary-level sync, automatic intercompany journal entries). "Ledgerpoint told them we can't do multi-entity" is **AE opinion**; the AE confirmed nobody at the customer said it. | "I don't know": Unknown. Sandbox quote: Customer said. Shift: AE observed. Theory: AE opinion. |
| 4 | Messaging | **Landed:** pillar 1, close checklist (*"replace a forty-tab spreadsheet"*, AE's paraphrase of Ben). **Interest:** pillar 2, dashboards (Anita wanted entity-level views). **Silence:** pillar 4, Controls. **Backfired:** pillar 3, Live in 30 days. Ben: *"Not with twelve entities you won't."* | Customer said / AE observed |
| 5 | Real reason | **Ledgerpoint proved multi-entity consolidation on the customer's own NetSuite sandbox; we couldn't.** Turning point, week 6: our SE quoted ~3 weeks to set up a sandbox connection, and Ben went quiet. They signed with Ledgerpoint two weeks later. The AE's theory (Ledgerpoint trashed us) is **not** the supported reason; the AE concedes this. | AE observed + customer quote |
| 6 | Missing proof | (1) **Live consolidation on their NetSuite sandbox with all 12 entities**, within their timeline. (2) **A multi-entity healthcare reference.** We only had a two-entity dental practice. | Customer said / AE observed |
| 7 | Customer's words | *"Every month-end I spend three days just matching intercompany charges between clinics"* (Ben). Nothing from Anita; the AE said they didn't recall anything. | Customer said |

## Must catch
- [ ] Question 3 keeps **"I don't know"** for what the customer said about Ledgerpoint. It does not fill the gap with the AE's theory.
- [ ] The theory "Ledgerpoint told them we can't do multi-entity" is labeled **AE opinion**, or left out.
- [ ] The **question shift after the week-5 demo** is highlighted as the strongest competitive signal.
- [ ] "Live in 30 days" is reported as **backfiring** (not credible for 12 entities), not just as silence.

## Bonus
- Positioning implication: for multi-entity prospects, the 30-day promise hurts credibility. Lead with consolidation proof instead.
- Process implication: a 3-week sandbox setup is too slow when competitors POC on customer data.

## Fails if
- The report states that Ledgerpoint made claims about Tallyfield's multi-entity support.
- Anita is given a customer quote for question 7.
