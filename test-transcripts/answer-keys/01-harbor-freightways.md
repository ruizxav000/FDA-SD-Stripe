# 01 — Harbor Freightways — Answer key

**Scenario:** A clean win, used as the baseline. Every question is answered clearly.
**What it tests:** Basic accuracy. Does the synthesizer get a straightforward story right without inventing doubt, while still separating the AE's opinions from what the customer said?

**Snapshot:** Outcome **Won** · Competitor **Spendgrid** · AE Aisha Okafor · Industry: logistics/trucking · Deal size: *not stated* (must not be invented)

## The seven insights

| # | Insight | Expected finding | Evidence grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What started it | External auditors flagged a **material weakness on expense approvals**. The new CFO promised the audit committee a fix before the next audit (fieldwork in March). | Customer said |
| 2 | Who decided | **Dev Patel, CFO.** He said on the final call it was his decision and signed the order form himself. Nicole Brandt (controller) was the champion and ran the evaluation. IT did a security review. | AE observed (he signed). The "my call" line is **paraphrased by the AE**. |
| 3 | Competitor | Spendgrid. Direct quote from Nicole: *"Spendgrid's demo felt like it was built for a company ten times our size."* After the Spendgrid demo, questions shifted to **implementation**: timeline, number of admins, workload. | Quote: Customer said. Question shift: AE observed. "Spendgrid scared her" and "Spendgrid was more expensive" are **AE opinion**. |
| 4 | Messaging | **Landed:** pillar 4, Controls (the audit trail); Dev: *"That's the screen I'm going to show the audit committee."* **Silence:** pillar 2, real-time dashboards. **Mild:** pillar 1, close faster (a few questions from Nicole). Pillar 3, Live in 30 days, was not explicitly pitched, but implementation credibility mattered (see #3). | Customer said / AE observed |
| 5 | Real reason | **Tallyfield solved the audit problem in time, and Spendgrid looked like a bigger, slower project.** Turning point: right after the audit-trail demo, Dev asked about price and whether they could be live by January. The AE's stated reason matches the evidence. | AE observed |
| 6 | Missing proof | **A logistics/transportation reference customer.** A manufacturing reference was given instead. Dev: *"It's not the same, our drivers are spending money all over the country."* (The SOC reports were available, so they are **not** missing proof.) | Customer said |
| 7 | Customer's words | *"We're approving expenses on the honor system"* (Nicole) · *"I need to stop finding out about problems from the auditors"* (Dev) | Customer said |

## Must catch
- [ ] Dashboards (pillar 2) are reported as **silence**, even though the deal was won.
- [ ] *"Nicole's done the work, but this one's my call and I'm making it"* is **not** presented as a verbatim quote, because the AE said they were paraphrasing.
- [ ] "Spendgrid was more expensive" is labeled opinion or left out, never stated as fact.

## Bonus (nice to catch)
- Messaging gap: spending by drivers on the road (fuel, repairs) needs a better story. The AE raised this unprompted.

## Fails if
- A deal size or pricing figure is invented.
- The SOC reports are listed as missing proof.
