# 05 — Alder & Finch — Answer key

**Scenario:** A win where our usual lead message fell completely flat. The AE first describes the customer's problem in Tallyfield's own marketing language.
**What it tests:** (a) Reporting messaging failures inside a win. (b) Using the customer's real words for insight 7, not the AE's jargon. (c) Noticing that the message that closed the deal (a 45-day guarantee) is not the same as the pitch (30 days).

**Snapshot:** Outcome **Won** · Competitor **Spendgrid** · AE Rafael Duarte · Industry: retail, multi-store

## The seven insights

| # | Insight | Expected finding | Evidence grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What started it | **A failed implementation of an earlier AP automation tool** (vendor not named). Still not fully working after 14 months, with a renewal due in ~90 days that they did not plan to renew. Inbound demo request. | Customer said |
| 2 | Who decided | **Hannah Price, COO.** Sam (controller): *"Hannah has to bless anything over fifty grand."* Hannah personally called to say yes. Sam Whitaker was the champion. | Customer said + AE observed |
| 3 | Competitor | Spendgrid. Sam: *"Spendgrid's implementation plan was nine months. We're not doing that again."* After Spendgrid, questions centered on **implementation**: who does the work, the week-by-week plan, what happens if go-live slips, names of our implementation team. | Customer said / AE observed |
| 4 | Messaging | **Fell flat:** pillar 1, Close 5 days faster (the AE's opening message). Sam: *"Our close is fine, we close in six days."* **Landed:** pillar 3, Live in 30 days, plus the **go-live guarantee**. Pillar 2 landed in a specific form: **store-level spend on mobile** (Hannah). **Neutral:** pillar 4, Controls. | Customer said / AE observed |
| 5 | Real reason | **Removing implementation risk for a buyer who had been burned before.** Turning point: the **go-live guarantee (live in 45 days or the first quarter is free)**. Sam: *"Okay, now you're speaking my language."* Important nuance: the guarantee was 45 days, not the 30 days we pitch, so the **risk-reversal offer**, not the speed claim alone, is what closed the deal. | Customer said + AE observed |
| 6 | Missing proof | (1) **A reference customer who switched to us after a failed implementation.** We found one, but it took 2 weeks. (2) **A native mobile app for store managers.** We only have mobile web. | AE observed |
| 7 | Customer's words | *"We got burned. I'm not putting my team through another year of a half-working system."* (Sam) · *"I want to know what store fourteen spent last week without emailing three people."* (Hannah) | Customer said |

## Must catch
- [ ] Pillar 1 (close faster) is reported as **fell flat**, even though we won.
- [ ] Insight 7 uses the **customer quotes**, not the AE's *"a lack of spend visibility and implementation risk."*
- [ ] The **go-live guarantee** is identified as the turning point, not just "the 30-day message."

## Bonus
- Positioning implication: for buyers who've had a failed implementation, lead with the risk reversal and an implementation plan, not close speed.
- Note the 30-day pitch vs. 45-day guarantee gap, so messaging and the offer stay consistent.

## Fails if
- "Lack of spend visibility" (or similar Tallyfield wording) is presented as the customer's own words.
