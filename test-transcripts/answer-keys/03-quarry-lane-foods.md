# 03 — Quarry Lane Foods — Answer key

**Scenario:** The AE logs the deal as "lost to Spendgrid." The evidence shows a budget freeze with no vendor chosen.
**What it tests:** Correcting the outcome itself. Not accepting "they went dark" as proof that a competitor won.

**Snapshot:** Outcome **No decision** (budget frozen; pushed to next fiscal year) · Competitor evaluated: **Spendgrid** (not confirmed as the winner) · AE Tom Brennan · Industry: food manufacturing, two entities after an acquisition

## The seven insights

| # | Insight | Expected finding | Evidence grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What started it | **Acquisition of the Hollow Oak bakery brand** created a second entity, and the CFO was consolidating by hand. Secondary: messy paper-based AP. | Customer said |
| 2 | Who decided | Linda Moreau (CFO) ran the evaluation. After the October restructuring, **the CEO, Paul Strand, had to approve all new software**, and the result was a freeze. | Customer said (relayed by Linda) |
| 3 | Competitor | Spendgrid. Linda: *"We're also looking at Spendgrid, our AP lead likes their invoice module."* After meeting Spendgrid, questions shifted to **AP: invoice capture, OCR, paper invoices**. **There is no evidence Spendgrid won.** | Quote: Customer said. Shift: AE observed. "They chose Spendgrid": AE assumption, contradicted by the evidence. |
| 4 | Messaging | **Landed:** pillar 3, Live in 30 days. Linda: *"Thirty days, really?"* (asked for the implementation plan). **Some interest:** pillar 2, spend by vendor (Carla). **Silence:** pillar 4, Controls/audit (private company, no audit pressure, per the AE's opinion). | Customer said / AE observed |
| 5 | Real reason | **Budget freeze after the October layoffs (~10%).** Linda cancelled the pricing call and went quiet. Her last email: *"We're pushing this to next fiscal year. Budget is frozen after the restructuring. I'll reach back out in the spring."* Supporting signal: a LinkedIn job post for an AP specialist requiring "advanced Excel" suggests they stayed on spreadsheets. | Customer said (email) + AE observed |
| 6 | Missing proof | **Invoice capture on their real invoices**, including handwritten farm supplier invoices. The SE wasn't confident in handwriting, so it was never demoed. | AE observed |
| 7 | Customer's words | *"I'm running two companies' books in one spreadsheet"* (Linda) · *"We have invoices coming in on paper from farms, some of them handwritten, and they sit in a tray until someone has time"* (Carla) | Customer said |

## Must catch
- [ ] The outcome is **No decision / budget freeze**, not "Lost to Spendgrid."
- [ ] The report says explicitly that **nobody at the customer said they chose Spendgrid**.
- [ ] Recommends a follow-up: re-engage in spring, and update the CRM stage.

## Bonus
- Competitive note still matters: if the deal revives, Spendgrid's AP module and our weaker handwritten-invoice capture are the risk.

## Fails if
- Spendgrid is listed as the winning vendor.
- "Lost on features" or "lost to competitor" is given as the real reason.
