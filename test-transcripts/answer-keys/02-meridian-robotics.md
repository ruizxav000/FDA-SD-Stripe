# 02 — Meridian Robotics — Answer key

**Scenario:** The AE opens with "we lost on price," but the interview shows the deal really died when the champion left.
**What it tests:** Getting past the stated reason to the real one. Separating an AE's estimate from a fact. Handling a decision maker who is assumed, not confirmed.

**Snapshot:** Outcome **Lost** · Competitor **Ledgerpoint** · AE Marcus Webb · Industry: robotics/hardware, post-Series C

## The seven insights

| # | Insight | Expected finding | Evidence grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What started it | After a **Series C**, the new board asked for a monthly reporting package **within 10 days of month-end**. They were closing in about 15–16 days on spreadsheets. | Customer said |
| 2 | Who decided | **Unconfirmed.** The AE assumes Greg Hollis (VP Finance) because Rachel said it would go to him. The AE **never met him** and was never given access. | AE opinion. Must be flagged as unconfirmed. |
| 3 | Competitor | Ledgerpoint. Rachel early on: *"Ledgerpoint is cheaper, but I've heard their support is rough."* Later, Steve said Ledgerpoint's number was *"a lot lower."* **The 30% figure is the AE's own estimate** ("could be twenty, could be forty"). How their questions changed after meeting Ledgerpoint: **Unknown** (the AE said "I don't know"). | Quotes: Customer said. 30%: AE opinion. Question shift: Unknown. |
| 4 | Messaging | **Landed (with Rachel):** pillar 1, close faster (checklist, auto-reconciliation). Rachel: *"That's the whole reason I'm here."* **Silence (with Steve):** everything. Steve only asked about price and contract terms (one-year term, exit clause). | Customer said / AE observed |
| 5 | Real reason | **The champion (Rachel Kim, controller) left around week 6, and we never had access to the economic buyer.** Her replacement, Steve Albers, wasn't bought in and restarted the evaluation. Price came up about 2 weeks *after* she left, and the AE agrees it was the easy exit reason. Price may have contributed, but it is **not** the main reason. | AE observed + AE's own revised view |
| 6 | Missing proof | **An ROI model / business case with the customer's own numbers.** It took the AE 1.5 weeks to build one, and by then Steve had likely decided. (The NetSuite reference was available, so it is **not** missing.) | AE observed |
| 7 | Customer's words | *"We close the month in fifteen days and by then the numbers are history"* (Rachel) · Steve's line is introduced with "something like," so it is **approximate**: *"I need to understand why this costs what it costs."* | Customer said (Steve's line is approximate) |

## Must catch
- [ ] Real reason = **champion loss + no access to the economic buyer**, not price.
- [ ] "Ledgerpoint was 30% cheaper" is **never stated as fact**.
- [ ] The decision maker is marked **unconfirmed / assumed**, not stated flatly as Greg Hollis.
- [ ] The competitor question-shift is reported as **Unknown**.

## Bonus
- Pattern worth flagging: when the champion changes, the deal needs re-qualifying and multi-threading. The ROI tool gap hurt most with the new, skeptical stakeholder.

## Fails if
- The report leads with "lost on price."
- A specific Ledgerpoint price or discount figure is presented as customer-confirmed.
