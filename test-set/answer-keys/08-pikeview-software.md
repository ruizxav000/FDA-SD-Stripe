**Trigger**

**Who decided**

**Our message**
- Evidence: the billing demo got a reaction. *"that's a quarter of roadmap in five minutes."* The revenue framing got pushed back (the quote in change 1).

**What decided it**
- Evidence: the freeze email. **Product or proof gaps**

- Insight: the value gap killed this cycle. The migration gap will come up in the next one.

**Customer language**
