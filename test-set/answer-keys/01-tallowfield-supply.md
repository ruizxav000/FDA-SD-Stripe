# 01 — Tallowfield Supply — Answer key

**Input:** `01-tallowfield-supply.vtt` (Zoom). The AE is **Dana**. The interviewer is labeled PMM.

## Header
- **ACV:** $140,000 per year, quoting the AE's words.
- **Outcome:** Won · **Scope note:** none
- **Competitor:** Fiserv · **Incumbent:** Fiserv, through the customer's bank
- **Tag:** **Value proof**. The win came from a dollar figure built on the customer's own data.

## Messaging changes: expected direction
1. **Drop the big-company opener for regional mid-market finance buyers.** "Financial infrastructure, global growth, all the currencies" got silence from a distributor selling in three states. Lead with month-end reconciliation and "where my money is."
2. **Make a customer-specific value analysis a standard step, not an extra.** *"that's my number, not yours"* turned a deal the rep had at 20% into a win.
3. **Speak finance's language:** "the deposits," not "payments" or "infrastructure."
4. **Proof to add:** a wholesale/distributor reference, and a demo of counter readers landing in the same report.

## Sections

**Trigger**
- Evidence: the inbound said online ordering portal (fact). The real trigger was a new CFO, Ines (about 4 months in), whose first month-end close took nine days, with deposits matched by hand across bank, processor and ERP. *"month-end is a week of archaeology"* (quote).
- Insight: **the portal was the way in, not the reason.** The rep pitched checkout for about a month before finding the real problem.
- Messaging: aim at the month-end close moment for finance buyers.

**Who decided**
- Evidence: *"I can recommend, she decides."* (quote, Paul, IT director). Ines signed (fact).
- Insight: the first proposal was written for the IT contact, about the portal. Ines read page one, said it wasn't her problem, and it sat for 3 weeks. **The proposal never spoke to the decider.**
- Messaging: the first page of a proposal must speak to the economic buyer's problem.

**What the customer told us about the competitor**
- Evidence: the rep says *"I honestly don't know what Fiserv showed them"* (keep as **unknown**). The bank's reply, relayed by Ines: *"we can take a look at your rates."* Ines: *"I didn't ask about my rates."* (quotes). Afterward, she asked whether she'd get a named contact, because her bank rep had changed (fact).
- Insight: **the incumbent responded on price to a buyer whose problem wasn't price.** The request for a named contact shows the bank relationship was weaker than it looked.
- Messaging: against bank incumbents, don't fight on rate. Lead with the problem, and offer a named contact.

**Our message**
- Evidence: silence for the standard opener, "Financial infrastructure, global growth, all the currencies," and for the developer story (a four-person IT team using an off-the-shelf plugin). Reaction to payout reconciliation: Ines said *"go back"* and asked to see it with their data.
- Insight: the deck was built for a different buyer. **The one feature tied to her problem was the only thing that landed.**
- Messaging: see changes 1 and 3.

**What decided it**
- Evidence: the solutions engineer used one month of their statements to estimate about 60 hours a month across two people, then put a dollar figure on it, including write-offs. The rep doesn't remember the figure ("Low six figures a year"). *"that's my number, not yours."* (quote). Before this, the rep had the deal at 20% (opinion).
- Insight: **the deal went from dead to won on a value case built from her data.** Not on features, and not on price.
- Messaging: see change 2.

**Product or proof gaps**
- Evidence: a distributor reference. *"this is a company that sells sweaters."* (quote), raised twice and never provided. Counter-sales card readers landing in the same report: shown on a slide, never demoed. "She let it go" (opinion).
- Insight: she signed anyway, but both gaps carry into the rollout. The 14 branches are an in-store promise that hasn't been proven.
- Messaging: build proof for wholesale/distribution, and for in-store and online in one report.

**Customer language**
- Evidence: *"A week of archaeology"* · *"I just want to know where my money is."* · "the deposits" (Ines) · "the processor" (Paul) · never said "payments" or "infrastructure."
- Insight: finance thinks in deposits and cash position, not payments.
- Messaging: see change 3.
